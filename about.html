How we inspect

Store owners are being asked to connect AI tools to their live customer and payment data faster than anyone can reasonably vet them. This is the vetting.

What we check on every tool

  • Publisher identity — is this the actual platform, a known company, or an anonymous account?
  • Login method — proper sign-in (OAuth) versus pasting in a raw key versus, in the worst cases, being asked for your password directly (an automatic fail).
  • Permission scope — can it only read your data, or can it also change things? Both are labeled plainly, not buried in fine print.
  • Maintenance — is it still being actively updated, or abandoned?

What the score means

Each tool gets a score from 1–10. Below 6 is flagged as a risk with a specific reason given — never a vague warning. We'd rather list fewer tools than list unclear ones.

How this site makes money

Some listed tools are affiliate partners — meaning if you sign up through our link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects the score: a tool is scored the same whether or not it pays us anything, and any sponsored or featured placement is labeled clearly as such, separate from the inspection score.

Something wrong?

If a tool's setup flow changed, or you think a score is wrong, tell us — this directory is only useful if it stays accurate.
