How an inspection works

  1. 01

    Confirm the publisher

    Is it the actual platform (Shopify, Stripe) or an anonymous account? We check who's really behind it.

  2. 02

    Check the login method

    Proper sign-in (OAuth) beats pasting in a raw key — and anything asking for your password outright is rejected.

  3. 03

    Read vs. write

    Can it only look at your data, or can it actually change things — refund, edit, send? We label this clearly.

  4. 04

    Check it's maintained

    Abandoned tools accumulate unpatched holes. We check for recent, active upkeep.
